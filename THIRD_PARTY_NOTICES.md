## Apps4Av Chart Metadata

Aerobag's chart cutline collection began with data adopted from the Apps4Av
Avare source tree. Aerobag now maintains the collection, including substantial
cutline corrections and additional chart-layout metadata, under
`product/chart-metadata/`.

The inherited GeoJSON remains subject to the Apps4Av license, recorded locally
as `LicenseRef-Apps4Av` in `LICENSES/LicenseRef-Apps4Av.txt`. Aerobag's
modifications are additionally licensed under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.

See `product/chart-metadata/UPSTREAM.md` for the upstream project and detailed
provenance.
